# Privacy notice draft — not legal approval

The current Nardora local-play build stores preferences, unfinished-match
state, and local statistics on the user's device. It does not require an
account and does not intentionally transmit gameplay or personal data to a
Nardora backend. GitHub Pages, a future app store, an optional platform SDK,
and the operating system may process technical data under their own terms.

Before release, the owner must confirm every enabled SDK and permission,
complete the Apple privacy manifest and Google Play Data safety answers, name
the controller/contact, define retention and deletion paths, add effective and
updated dates, and obtain jurisdiction-appropriate legal review. Supabase or
analytics must not be described as active until their real production
configuration and data flows are verified.
