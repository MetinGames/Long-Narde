## Description

Nardora brings Long Narde to your device with multiple bot levels, optional
turn timers, undo, quick bear-off, themes, and sound controls. Local matches do
not require an account or network connection.

Online tables, real-player accounts, and in-app purchases are not promised by
this build. Revalidate this copy against the signed package, screenshots, and
support URL before store submission.
